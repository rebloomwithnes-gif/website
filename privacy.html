Privacy

How I look after your details.

You're trusting me with something personal. This page explains, in plain words, what I keep, why, who helps me, and how to have it deleted.

Last updated: November 2026

Who I am

Rebloom is run by me, Nes. I decide how your details are used, so I'm responsible for looking after them.

Any question, or to see, change or delete your details: hello@rebloomwithnes.co.uk. You can also reply to any email I've sent you.

What I collect

  • When you claim a spot: your email address.
  • If you answer the next questions: your first name, the area you'd like to start with, which pattern sounds most like you, why now (if you share it), how you'd like me to call you, and your phone or WhatsApp number (if you share it).
  • If you send a message: your name (if you give it), your email address and what you write.
  • If you join a group: your daily check-in photos and messages in the group, and what you share on our calls.

Please don't share health details in the forms. If something comes up on a call, I won't write it down unless you ask me to.

Why I use it

  • To reply to you and arrange our 15-minute call. You've asked me to, and I need your details to do it.
  • To send you the mini-guide and occasional emails about Rebloom. Only because you gave me your email for this. Reply "unsubscribe" any time and I'll stop.
  • To run your 30 days. Setting up your group, your buddy, your progress and our Sunday reflections.

I never sell your details, and I don't use them for advertising.

Who helps me

A few services help me run Rebloom. They only use your details to do that job for me.

  • Cloudflare hosts this website and runs the forms.
  • Resend sends the emails and keeps the sign-up list.
  • Google Calendar is where you book our call.
  • WhatsApp is where the groups happen. Everyone in your group can see your WhatsApp name, profile photo and phone number. You can hide your profile photo in WhatsApp's own privacy settings.
  • Instagram, if you message me there.

Some of these companies store data outside the UK. When they do, they use the legal safeguards UK law requires.

Your photos and the group

Only your small group, your buddy and I see your photos. We all agree that what's shared in the group stays in the group, and that nobody screenshots or shares another person's photos or messages.

You can delete your own photos and messages in WhatsApp at any time, and you can leave the group whenever you like.

How long I keep it

I keep your details while you're on the list or taking part, and for up to 12 months after we were last in touch, so I can follow up on your next season. After that I delete them. You can ask me to delete them sooner at any time.

Cookies

This website doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies, and doesn't follow you around the internet.

Your rights

You can ask me to:

  • show you the details I hold about you
  • correct anything that's wrong
  • delete your details
  • stop emailing you, or stop using your details

Just email hello@rebloomwithnes.co.uk. I'll reply within a month, and usually much sooner.

If you're unhappy with how I've handled your details, please tell me first so I can put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113.

Rebloom isn't therapy or medical advice. If you're struggling right now, Samaritans are free, any time, on 116 123 (UK). In an emergency, call 999.
